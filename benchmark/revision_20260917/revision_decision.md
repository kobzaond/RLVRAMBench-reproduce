# Completed independent-control experiment and manuscript decision

The frozen experiment resolves all 48 configurations from 144 invocations:
36 within-margin configurations and twelve memory-failing configurations.
Every three-seed configuration agrees. There are no unresolved or retried
invocations. The total outer-allocation cost is 50.54 GPU-hours.

The seven classifier/combined-rule disagreements selected before execution
all complete within margin. The classifier avoids no additional erroneous
approval by rejecting them. Its earlier weights and all unfavorable results
are retained. Refitting on this panel and rescoring it would not constitute
new evidence of transfer, so no such update is presented.

The revised article leads with controlled configuration measurements.
Independent variation identifies the offload–reservation completion pattern;
matched peak comparisons show stage reductions that do not translate directly
into an equal whole-run reduction. All 36 failures are generation-engine
startup-budget rejections, not failed actor updates. The rule is explicitly
limited to the selected discrete grid and one-step execution.

The main TeX now puts configuration controls before prediction comparisons,
adds both outcome and decision tables, compresses the older larger-model
narrative, and moves four supporting tables into existing supplementary
sections. No supplementary section was added. The abstract, discussion,
conclusion, and dataset instructions reflect the new result.

The research does not require another GPU campaign for this bounded claim.
A claim about an improved classifier, sustained larger-model training, another
hardware type, or another implementation would require additional evaluation.
Acceptance probability is not a scientifically verifiable release criterion.

The final critical review requested explicit one-step scope in the abstract
and methods, precise distinction between global and micro-batches, observed
rather than general necessity, stage-maximum rather than allocation-ownership
interpretation, and stable supplementary table references. These corrections
were applied directly to the TeX. The weight-synchronization interpretation
was checked against all 36 offloaded lower-reservation runs. The reviewer
identified no need for new experiments after those bounded corrections.

The original watcher failure and all frozen records remain untouched.
CPU reconstruction from relocated raw records and the archived execution
source matches all six outputs with the live project and reference outcomes
hidden. Final test, compilation, source-package, and artifact reports accompany
this revision. Author-controlled submission declarations remain separate.
